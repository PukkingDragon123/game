Otter Village

A cozy island life — fish, sail, build & befriend

📖 How to play
🕹️ Move: drag on the left (or WASD). ⚡ Dash: button, Shift/Space, or double-tap.
🤚 Do (E): tap the green button (or press E) for everything — chop 🌳 trees & mine 🪨 rocks, build, fish, read 📬 mail, recruit villagers.
🤲 Carry & build: you can hold 3 resources at once. Stand on an empty plot → pick a blueprint → carry 🪵 wood & 🪨 rock to the 🚧 site to build it!
🦪 Pearls are your money — earn them from fishing & treasure.
🍳 Cook: gather 🫐 berries & 🍎 fruit, catch 🐟 fish, then cook at the 🍳 Kitchen — the aroma charms new villagers to move in!
🎣 Fishing: hook a fish, then tap REEL! fast — the bar drains as it fights back. Fill it to land the catch!
⛵ Sail to 🏘️ Village Isle (recruit villagers) & 🏴‍☠️ Treasure Isle. Dive (🤿/F) for reef fish & treasure.
