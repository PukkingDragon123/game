Otter Village

Dive the reef, catch fish & grow your cozy village

🕹️ Move: drag on the left (or WASD). ⚡ Dash: button, Shift/Space, or double-tap.
🤿 Dive: tap the mask on open water to explore the reef (or press F).
💨 Oxygen: air drains underwater & dashing costs extra — surface in time!
🎒 Inventory: press E (or tap 🎒) for your backpack — drag to sort, 1–8 picks a hotbar slot. Build a 📦 Chest for storage.
💎 Treasure: grab hidden chests & gems on the seabed for 💰 coins.
🏗️ Build & 🌷 Decorate: place huts, houses & décor on the tile map (Build/Decor tabs).
🛠️ Edit mode: stand on an item & tap ♻️ to pick it up (full refund). Tap a 🏠 house to repaint it.
🏝️ Buy Land with coins to grow the island & 🐾 adopt pets!
