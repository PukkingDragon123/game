Otter Village

Ride the waves, hunt fish & grow your cozy village

🕹️ Move: drag anywhere on the left (or use WASD / arrow keys).
💧 Gather: swim into floating treasures to scoop them up.
🐟 Hunt: chase the darting fish — they flee, so use 🥅 Net & 💨 Dash!
🐾 Hire: tap 🐾 to recruit sea lions, seals & walruses that auto-gather for you.
🏗️ Build: tap a building in the dock to plant it on the island.
⭐ Grow: everything earns XP — level up your village!
